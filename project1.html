BCIT Capstone project

During my last term at BCIT, I worked as the modeling lead on a capstone project, which was to create a custom 3D printed air intake for an internal combustion engine. I modeled the parts and created the assembly of the intake. Due to the geometric constraints of the engine bay, I had to find practical design solutions to ensure proper fitment, while designing around the manufacturing limitations of 3D printing. I also created manufacturing drawings with accurate dimensions and tolerances for each part of the intake. the final model was assembled, documented properly, and successfully presented at BCIT’s Mechanical Engineering Expo.
